PLANT OPERATIONS

Crusher Plant Operations & Process

The crusher plant is supported by an established crushing, screening, and conveying setup with heavy-duty feeders/hoppers, crushing equipment, vibrating screening, belt conveyors, and electrical control panels.

Step-by-Step Production Process

01

Raw Material Feed

Receiving and controlled feeding of stone / rock material into heavy hoppers.

02

Primary Crushing

Initial size reduction of feed material through high-capacity jaw crushers.

03

Secondary / Fine Crushing

Further size reduction, shaping, and precision aggregate calibration.

04

Screening

Multi-deck separation into required fractions (10mm, 20mm, 40mm, stone dust).

05

Conveying

Transfer of processed material continuously between plant sections via belts.

06

Stockpiling / Dispatch

Collection, storage, loader filling, and fast weighing dumper dispatch.
